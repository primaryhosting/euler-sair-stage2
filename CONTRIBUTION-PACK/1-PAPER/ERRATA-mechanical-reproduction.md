## Corrections

1. **§9 — SAIR Stage 2 chronology.** The PDF states the challenge ran "between
   March and April 2026." This is incorrect. Per the official Stage 2 overview,
   Stage 2 opened for **preregistration on 2026-04-23**, **started 2026-05-01**,
   and **closes 2026-08-31 (AoE)**. Source:
   `https://github.com/SAIRcompetition/equational-theories-lean-stage2/blob/main/rules/overview.md`.

2. **§ access history — sourcing.** The access-history discussion draws in part on
   private correspondence and personal observation. It is qualified in the text;
   for formal public release these passages should either cite dated documentary
   evidence or be shortened to a neutral summary. Until then, treat those specific
   claims as author testimony, not documented fact.
