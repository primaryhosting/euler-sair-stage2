# Four Tellings of One Result

Thurston reported that a step conveyable to topologists in two minutes took
an hour with analysts, and drew the conclusion this folder is built on:
reactivation is not a property of a text. It is a relation between a text
and a reader.

The paper this pack accompanies turns that observation into a test. Among
its five public reactivation criteria stands **translation**: explain the
result at different levels of expertise, and passing raises the probability
that a reader can perform a new act of understanding — which is the only
thing "understanding" has ever meant here.

This folder is that test, executed. One result — a solver for equational
implication whose every answer is a machine-checked certificate, and one
pivotal proof inside it — told four times, to four audiences, each telling
complete at its own altitude:

| | Telling | Audience | What survives the translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | **The Machine That Shows Its Work** | A curious twelve-year-old, or anyone | The whole shape of the thing, with no notation at all |
| II | **The Table, the Worlds, and the Judge** | The scientifically literate reader | The ideas, with just enough notation to be honest |
| III | **A Practitioner's Account** | Formal-methods and automated-reasoning people | The architecture, the numbers with their badges, the failures |
| IV | **The Formal Account** | The referee Tao's criterion imagines | Sufficiency and necessity: precise claims, actual derivations, exact provenance — nothing that cannot be checked, nothing needed that is absent |

One discipline holds across all four. The same pivotal proof — that law
1689 forces a magma to collapse to a point, from which law 2391 follows in
one line — appears in every telling, at four depths: as a story, as a
sketch, as a derivation, and as the verbatim kernel-checked text. If the
translations are faithful, a reader who climbs from I to IV should feel the
same proof come into focus rather than meet a different one.

And one warning, which is the paper's: a telling is not the trace. These
documents are retrospective exposition, written to be lucid. The
contemporaneous record — what actually failed, in what order, on what dates
— lives in `8-TRACE/` and the reactivation packet, and nothing here is
evidence of anything. The evidence is checkable; these are invitations to
check it.
