02 · 12

The problem

A magma is the most lawless object in algebra.

A set, one binary operation ◇, no axioms. An equational law is a universally quantified identity. Given two laws, decide:

does every magma satisfying   E1   also satisfy   E2 ?

Not usually. Not on the worlds we checked. Every magma — including ones no person will think of. FALSE needs a finite counterexample table; TRUE needs a Lean proof. No partial credit. The judge's verdict is the only score.

03 · 12

Why it matters

Correct, checked — and not yet knowledge.

Machines now write proofs no human has read. Terence Tao's criterion: a proof that no human can properly explain should be viewed as incomplete — even when a computer has formally verified it.

This setting makes verification free and perfect, and thereby moves all the interest to the questions verification cannot answer. That is exactly where the accompanying paper — Mathematics in the Age of Mechanical Reproduction — argues the epistemics now live. EULER is one small system built under that criterion, on purpose.

Architecture · a refusal stack

Look things up. Don't trust cleverness. Don't stop at one method.

oraclethe published implication closure — direction, exact, no guessing

TRUE · completeKnuth–Bendix — total emission: if it emits, the Lean is valid

No answer counts unless the judge accepts it. The deterministic tiers self-check before emitting; the grind and model tiers are unchecked guesses gated only by the judge. No code path leads from a heuristic guess to a judge-accepted answer.

06 · 12

Where search ends and seeing begins

Watch a world collapse.

h : ∀ x y z, x = (y ◇ x) ◇ ((x ◇ z) ◇ z) — law 1689. Completion derives L10 : a = a ◇ b. The machine held the lever and could not finish: rewriting cannot say “all elements are equal.” A human saw what kind of statement the goal was, and closed it in three lines. The kernel checked both, and cared for neither who wrote what.

07 · 12

The honesty budget

What generalizes, and what is only a ceiling.

Named, not hidden

  • The released-set sweep leans on pair-keyed certificates — disclosed per pair, never counted as generalization.
  • The FALSE bank's harvest targeted the released sets; the held-out numbers are the claim of record.
  • The two long-unresolved public FALSE pairs are now closed — an infinite ℤ model and a finite order-13 model, built by Aristotle and verified on the judge's Lean 4.32.2. One is provably infinite-only; the other just sat above our search bound.

Three fidelity failures, on the record

A goal-binding mismatch that accepted proofs the judge rejected; an operator glyph (* vs ◇) that rejected five correct proofs; and — caught in a third adversarial audit — a sibling solver whose FALSE certificates were sound tables but omitted three Lean imports, so every one would have failed to compile. Each time the checker was flawless; what failed was fidelity between what we emitted and what we meant. No verifier guards that gap. It deserves the daylight — and a compile on the judge's own toolchain now catches it.

08 · 12

The field · two philosophies, one judge

Creativity gated by verification, or mathematics precomputed.

The other school

A language model steers mechanically-verified tools — creative lemma bridges, treated as untrusted hints and proven before use. Genuinely sound. Hostage to a weak proxy model, and blind on direction.

Ours

An exact direction oracle from public data, deterministic provers, disclosed certificates. Both end at the same deterministic judge. The difference is reproducibility: EULER's every result regenerates without any model in the loop, from public materials, on a laptop.

That is not a competitive tactic. It is the paper's thesis, instantiated — with a live empirical contrast in the same competition.

09 · 12

Standing on shoulders · attribution is part of the epistemics

Two systems this work would look different without.

Axle — Axiom

Built by Carina Hong and the Axiom Math team. The dev-time verification backbone: judge-exact Lean compile checks in about two seconds instead of a forty-minute round trip. Every certificate passed through it — and both fidelity failures were discovered as Axle divergences.

Aristotle — Harmonic

Harmonic's Lean theorem prover proved 390 of the solver's hard TRUE implications (traced to dated May-2026 jobs), embedded and disclosed, re-verified by the judge — a dev-time contributor, never called at runtime. As an independent upper bound it also proved the same 50/50 held-out TRUE as EULER's runtime, and constructed the two countermodels that closed the last FALSE holes.

With the Equational Theories Project (Tao et al.), and the classical stack — Knuth–Bendix, Twee, Mace4, E, Vampire. None a runtime dependency; all shaped what the runtime carries.
